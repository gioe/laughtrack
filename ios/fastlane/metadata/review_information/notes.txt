Laughtrack is a live comedy and podcast discovery app. On first launch, choose Continue as guest to browse without an account, or continue with Apple or Google. Sign-in is required for saved shows and favorites; it is not required for browsing or adding an upcoming show to the device calendar.

The app intentionally uses a dark appearance.

To test:
1. Open Discover. Choose a location or use location access for nearby shows. Location permission is optional; manual location selection and Search remain available.
2. Open Search and select Shows, Comedians, Clubs, or Podcasts. Search by name and use the available filters.
3. Open a show for lineup, venue, and ticket information. Ticket links open an external ticketing website; the app does not sell tickets.
4. For an upcoming show, use Add to calendar. The app requests calendar permission and adds the event to a writable device calendar.
5. Search for a podcast and open an episode. Episodes with an audio source can play in the app. Discover also recommends podcast episodes when qualifying content is available.
6. Sign in with Apple or Google to save a show or favorite a comedian, club, or podcast. View saved items in Library. Use the profile button at the top of Discover for account options.

Show listings, podcast recommendations, and playable audio depend on source availability. Store screenshots use curated sample content rendered by the app's actual screens.
