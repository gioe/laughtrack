As seen on HBO's "Crashing," The Grisly Pear is one of the hottest up-and-coming comedy clubs in the city. Booked by comics, for comics, the Grisly Pear therefore offers a unique insider perspective on the NYC comedy scene. Expect stars from Netflix, HBO, Comedy Central, and more, and the very best up and comers that you will brag you saw first. Even Marvelous Mrs. Maisel star Rachel Brosnahan came to the Grisly Pear for character research and to experience a gritty, authentic NYC club.
