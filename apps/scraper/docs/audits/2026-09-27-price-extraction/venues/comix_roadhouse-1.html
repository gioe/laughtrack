About

HEATHER SHAW is a seasoned stand-up comic, viral sensation, and podcast host whose explosive energy, sharp takes, and uncanny resemblance to Jim Carrey have made her one of the internet’s fastest-rising comedy stars. With more than 2.8 million followers across social media, HEATHER SHAW has built a massive audience through her wildly expressive characters, fearless crowd work, and viral commentary on relationships, pop culture, and everyday chaos. Her comedy and online presence have landed her appearances on The Today Show, Access Hollywood, and The Tamron Hall Show, while her stand-up career has seen her share stages with comics including Mark Normand, Eric André, and Fortune Feimster. HEATHER also hosts the weekly podcast Heather Shaw is Kidding, where she tears into current events, politics, and trending stories with her signature no-filter style. Fresh off a People Magazine feature surrounding her recent wedding, HEATHER SHAW continues turning viral fame into must-see live comedy.
