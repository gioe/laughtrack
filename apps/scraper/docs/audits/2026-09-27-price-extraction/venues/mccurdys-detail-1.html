Wyatt Cote’s charming, clever and quick witted anecdotes make him a fan favorite at every Club he performs in.
Wyatt is one of the best competitive comics in the country with five contest wins to his credit including The World Series of Comedy, Seattle International Comedy Competition and The San Francisco International Comedy Competition. His stand up has also appeared on Laughs on Fox and ABC news.
