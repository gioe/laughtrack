Location

Mary Stuart Rogers Theater

Name

Lucy Darling: All That and More

Description

This show is sold out or has only scattered single seats. If a seating chart appears below, you could choose from a very limited selection. If there is no seating chart, you may add your name to a waiting list by clicking here. Tickets aren’t likely to be available through the waiting list, though, so we highly recommend that you consider these other Comedy performances instead!

Award-winning performer Carisa Hendrix stars as charming socialite and cocktail enthusiast Lucy Darling as she continues her vivacious vagabond voyages with All That and More, a new experimental variety show featuring her razor-sharp wit, unquestionable advice, and skillful magic. 

For the first time in over a year, Lucy is joined by The Great Kaplan, a modern-day vaudevillian master. With an infinite assortment of unique and visually stunning skills, coupled with impeccable comedic timing, Kaplan joins Lucy to create a world where anything is possible.

Lucy is bringing her signature charm and mischief from screen to stage. All That and More is the perfect chance to take a break from reality and indulge in the mischief, sarcasm, and wonder that we all deserve. This is your chance to be part of the experimental beginnings of this brand new show.

5:30PM Pre-Show Talkback – $50 PER PERSON IN ADDITION TO A SHOW TICKET

CALL (209)338-2100 OR VISIT THE TICKET OFFICE

Enjoy a candid conversation with Carisa Hendrix (the performer behind Lucy Darling) two hours before showtime. Hear never-before-told stories, ask your own questions, and get a behind-the-scenes look at the artistry behind the performance.

Post-Show Meet & Greet – $150 PER PERSON IN ADDITION TO A SHOW TICKET

CALL (209)338-2100 OR VISIT THE TICKET OFFICE

Post-Show Meet & Greet includes meeting Lucy Darling after the show and taking a professional photograph with the icon herself. Guests will also receive a limited-edition lanyard and an exclusive merchandise gift to remember the night. The perfect cherry on top of an unforgettable evening.
