Matt Torres



A Chicago Native, Comedian, Actor, and Veteran of the Iraq War. Matthew has been through it all, which is how he developed his sense of humor. From the Southside of Chicago, to his time in the Marines. 


He has used Comedy as a tool to make the best out of life’s worst situations. Matt has also made appearances on such television shows as Justified, Shameless, and Criminal Minds.
