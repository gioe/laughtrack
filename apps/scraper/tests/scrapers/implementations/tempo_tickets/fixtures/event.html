ComedySportz is an all Improv, all suggestion based comedy match, fun for all ages. Two teams of players get suggestions from the audience, create scenes onstage, and compete for your laughs! You decide what each scene is about, you decide who you think is funnier, and you decide who wins! If you haven't guessed yet-- the most important part of the match is YOU!
Order tickets
It is assigned seating. If you're buying a single ticket and are part of a larger group, please use the group option! No refunds.
All shows have ASSIGNED SEATING. Please call 414-272-8888 if you are purchasing a single ticket but part of a larger group designating the group name. There is an option to check in your order to add the group name as well if you prefer. Additionally if you need more than ten tickets, do multiple orders under the same name, we'll be able to tell that you're part of the same group and make sure all tickets are seated with each other. ComedySportz is 100% Wheelchair accessible. We also can put up signs for your special events and celebrations! If you have special seating needs, or would like Us to help you celebrate with special announcements, please call our offices at 414-272-8888
