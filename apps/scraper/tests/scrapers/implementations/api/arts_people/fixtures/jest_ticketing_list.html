Brigadoon

June 5-August 30

Brigadoon is a timeless romantic musical filled with enchantment, adventure, and unforgettable music.

While traveling through the Scottish Highlands, two American friends, Tommy Albright and Jeff Douglas, stumble upon the mysterious village of Brigadoon — a place that appears on no map and seems untouched by the modern world. There, Tommy meets the beautiful Fiona, and a powerful love story unfolds. But Brigadoon holds a magical secret: the village comes to life for only one day every hundred years, and those who fall in love there must choose between the world they know and the world of their heart.

Featuring sweeping melodies, spirited dance numbers, and a story of love strong enough to defy time itself, Brigadoon invites audiences into a dreamlike world where romance, hope, and wonder reign supreme.

Front deRanged Improv Comedy

Roughly a decade ago, the OxyMorons improv troupe created a presence in Asheville, NC with standing-room-only comedy nights at the Area 45 Theatre. Three of OxyMorons' founding members now call Colorado home, and they've partnered with local talent to make funny Front deRanged style. Watch as players use your suggestions to create scenes, situations, and clever one-liners in an evening of unscripted, unbelievable - and dare we say unforgiving - comedy. It's a serious dose of funny that'll make you laugh until you cry (and that's no oxymoron).
