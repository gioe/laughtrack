Come and JAM Sundays @ UCB Franklin 5:30-6:30! Students of all levels are welcome! It’s free and a great way to get on stage and get reps!

An inclusive improv jam for UCB students, performers, and fans!

Anna Rajo, Joe Fahey, and PJ McCormick will host with a 4th special guest host every week. Come get some reps, make some friends, and be part of the UCB community.

The jam is free to attend, no reservation needed!!

—

Please arrive no later than 15 minutes after the show’s listed start time if you wish to be admitted and have an opportunity to join the jam.

Under 17 must be accompanied by a parent/guardian. Please use discretion, as all our shows can potentially use adult/sexual material, language, and images.

Lineup is subject to change.

Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre 5919 Franklin Avenue, Los Angeles, CA
