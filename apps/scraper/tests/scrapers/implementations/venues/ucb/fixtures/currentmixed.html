ASSSSCAT—UCB’s signature improv juggernaut—is a live improv comedy show in Los Angeles at the Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre. An all-star comedy show featuring comedians you know from SNL, Dropout, and the world of Film & Television doing improv inspired by true stories from a surprise celebrity guest. ASSSSCAT has played to critical acclaim and sold-out audiences across the country.

What Is ASSSSCAT?

It starts with a group of improv comedians taking the stage with a surprise celebrity guest. After getting a suggestion from the audience, our “monologist “ shares off-the-cuff thoughts, feelings, and true stories inspired by your suggestions. The improvisers then spin this monologue into a series of scenes that will show you why ASSSSCAT is one of the longest running improv shows in the world. Previous celebrity guests include: Will Ferrell, Mark Hamill, Tina Fey, Charles Barkley, Don Cheadle, Elizabeth Banks, Aubrey Plaza, Kumail Nanjiani, Amy Poehler, Robin Williams and more.
