3 teams, 2 UCB legend monologists, 1 student mashup, 0 regrets.

An improv show celebrating the Armando form and UCB’s legendary ASSSSCAT! Hosted by The Craft, this show brings together students, indie performers, and UCB legends!

Each show features four teams: The Craft, two invited improv teams, and a student mash-up team made up of performers who sign up at the show (names pulled from a bucket!). Two rotating UCB performers (teachers, alumni, house team members) join each month as the monologist and guest players!

Whether you’re a student looking to jump into the mash-up or a comedy fan who loves to laugh, come join us for a show that celebrates the awesome talent in the UCB community!

Hosted by The Craft: Bonnie Efird, Sarah Dooley, Lucy Hamilburg, Katie Roletto, Holly Celeste, Alex Paisley Stedman, Kate Bera, Leanne Velednitsky

Featuring…

The Seasoning: Capri Campeau, Tara Alise Cox, Da’rrel Stacy, Matthew Labayan, Nour Hajar, Jon Durnell

With monologists Ashley Ward & Greg Smith
